Midnight Pit uses FriendSDK v0.1.4 (Apache-2.0), including its chance-game rules, preview ledger, sound cues, and Generations pixel decoding.

Canonical Rare Friends artwork stays with the Generations NFT. Friend #777 is only the cached preview boss. Other Friend ids load canonical pixels from the public Robinhood RPC when it is reachable.
